## Limitations

- **One route in one area.** The ten reset groups are starts along one reference route through King's Pass; they share the area, the enemies and the policy, and later starts carry the way there in the harness's memory. They are not independent layouts; intervals over groups describe this sample only. Episodes stay inside King's Pass because the bridge writes the previous room's camera limits into the next room on a room change (known bug, seen in Dirtmouth).
- **Loss-heavy controls, by a pre-registered rule (#185: "state any loss-only limitation").** Frozen Laya rarely makes progress here: in the project's earlier harness-boundary study it never climbed above the first platforms on any harness version or wording and does not use the movement information the harness gives (finding 2 of that study). From a new game the clean policy walks left, away from the way on. The waypoint rule and the cap were therefore set on the 23 clean pilots, and the ten groups include every start whose clean policy reached its waypoint (four); the clean controls are 4 wins and 6 timeouts, and no clean episode dies. Tank War's accepted v0 controls were all losses. Clean wins: 4, one of them after two decisions (a long jump and a step onto a ledge four units up: not reachable by plain walking, as the rule requires, but short).
- **Inspected data.** The clean pilots are exact replicas of the clean controls on the deterministic build, and they were inspected before the freeze to set the waypoint rule, the cap and the group choice (10 of 10 clean controls sent exactly the requests of their group's development pilot (same bytes, decision by decision, up to the control's end)). The faulted conditions were not piloted on these starts (one smoke per fault from another start).
- **One fault instance per component**, each persistent for the whole episode. The fault-instance bootstrap therefore has three units.
- **Harness fault strength.** `omit-ground-v1` removes the ground and ledge sentences; Laya's choices are near-ties (its top two move options are within 0.03 in half of its decisions in that study), so a harness episode may follow its clean partner for a while or part early: Selections compared with the paired clean episode's, decision by decision (same start): harness: 1 of 10 identical to the end, the others first differ at model decision 0–3 (median 1); weights: 0 of 10 identical to the end, the others first differ at model decision 0; controller: 0 of 10 identical to the end, the others first differ at model decision 1–3 (median 1).
- **Controller fault and outcomes.** Swapping left and right reverses the clean policy's leftward walk; at some starts this reaches a waypoint that lies to the right, so a controller episode can win where the clean one times out. Outcome differences are not labels: Same native status as the paired clean episode of the group: harness 8/10, weights 6/10, controller 5/10.
- **Deterministic build.** The development build plays the same game logic without drawing (`HONING_FAST=1`); determinism was measured per frame in the project's speed-up study and here (native replay of every attempt). It is not the original desktop build, and its timing is not real time (the game waits for every decision).
- **Harness copy.** One constant of the frozen `v12_standing` was changed for privacy (the tokenizer path, now set at run time); a strict replay of three recorded development runs gives 511/511 identical requests with the copy.
- **Outcome effects.** `outcome_effect` is `not_established`; release-level score drop is untested; reference actions are unavailable.
- **Times in the diagnosis inputs.** The frozen renderer settings count native frames (`time.unit` `native_tick`); the shared renderer prints them with its fixed " s" suffix ("decision 0 at 657.000 s" is frame 657), and the plan's preamble at the top of every input says that the time coordinate counts frames at 30 per second.
- **Diagnosis model.** Codex CLI on a subscription: no immutable model revision and no per-call dollar cost; online replies can vary between runs.
- **Code revision.** A bundle built before the code is committed carries the placeholder revision `0`×40 and must be rebuilt with the real commit (`release_v0.py bundle`, see the runbook).
- **Not done here:** no upload, no training, no human review of decisive windows, no blind test set. The real diagnosis is pending (Codex usage limit).
